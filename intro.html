Everything you need to know before you start.

Is the free plan really free? +
Yes — no credit card, no trial period, no hidden fees. The free plan includes all SAT practice questions, vocabulary, mock exam links, and the daily challenge. Video courses require Pro.
Is the calculator the same as the real SAT? +
Yes. We embed Desmos Scientific, which is the exact same calculator used in the real digital SAT. Practicing with it means you'll be comfortable on exam day.
How are the questions made? +
Questions are written to match the exact format, difficulty, and topic distribution of the College Board's digital SAT — targeting the 1300–1600 score range. Every question includes a step-by-step explanation.
Does my progress save? +
Yes. Your account, progress, XP, streaks, errors, and vocabulary are all saved to your account using local storage. Cloud sync across devices is coming soon.
Can teachers upload courses? +
Yes. Email hello@shalgalt.mn and we'll review your content before publishing. The teacher upload portal is launching soon.
